## Team Charter

From the ... which can be reviewed in ["Appendix: Team Organization"](https://embedded-systems-design.github.io/EGR304TeamTemplate/Appendix/App-Team-Org/), the team's charter formed to be (showing ASU's Charter as an Example):

> Our organization is dedicated to developing a mathematically validated, theoretical PCB schematic and layout for an advanced PSU, managed entirely through a GitHub version-control workflow. Based on the RAS304 guidelines, our collaboration is driven by five shared goals that extend beyond a class grade: using this design experience to further our future careers, developing a standout addition to our engineering portfolios, demonstrating our technical knowledge to the broader community, furthering our shared passion for hardware, and making new professional connections. By uniting around these objectives and employing a strict peer-reviewed pull request system, we ensure our public-facing web documentation remains perfectly synchronized with a professional, theoretical hardware architecture.
